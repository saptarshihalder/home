So far

A somewhat incomplete history

A few things I remember more clearly than the rest.

2009 to 2020 School days

I spent most of my school years at Don Bosco in West Bengal. I liked computers, books, puzzles and anything that gave me something to figure out.

Most of it was ordinary in the best way. School, home, books, computers and years that felt slow until they were suddenly over.

Somehow, those are the days I remember best.

2020 to 2022 Science, lockdown and entrance exams

I picked science for senior secondary school and almost immediately the world shut down.

School shrank to the size of a screen, sleep schedules stopped making much sense and JEE, JEE Advanced, IAT, WBJEE and a few other exams took up a fairly unreasonable amount of my life.

Not my favourite era. Strange how far away it feels now.

2022 to now IISER Bhopal

I came to IISER Bhopal and, one rabbit hole at a time, ended up spending most of my days around data science, machine learning, mathematics and research.

University has mostly been a mixture of interesting ideas, unfinished side projects, questionable sleep and new subjects I suddenly decide I need to learn. Somewhere in between, the years started moving a little faster.

I am trying to notice more of it while I am still in it.

Research and internships
December 2023 to June 2024 IIIT Kalyani

This was one of my first proper research experiences. I worked remotely with Professor Imon Mukherjee and got a fairly early look at how messy research can actually be.

Ideas changed, experiments did not always work and the paper we hoped for never quite arrived. Still learned a lot, probably more than I realised at the time.

April 2024 to June 2024 Oxford

I worked with Professor Robin Evans from a few time zones away, learning about Markov models, Bayesian networks and graphical models.

It was one of those experiences where every answer somehow created three more questions.

May 2024 to July 2024 IIT BHU

My first offline research internship, and a summer away from home.

I worked around EEG signals, biometrics and machine learning, while also getting much better at figuring things out when nothing works the first time.

May 2024 to December 2024 Duke Health

I worked with John Thomas on removing artifacts from scalp EEG signals using deep learning.

It ended up being a nice mixture of neuroscience, signal processing, machine learning and many quiet hours spent looking at waveforms, trying to work out which squiggles were the brain and which were just someone blinking.

December 2024 to January 2025 Damodar Valley Corporation

My first proper corporate internship, over one winter.

I worked around cybersecurity, NIST frameworks, security processes and risk assessment.

Very different from academic research, which was probably exactly why it was useful.
